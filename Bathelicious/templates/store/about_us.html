Meet Our Founder

Where the idea began

Bathelicious was born from the vision of our founder, Lakshmi Varadarajan, a certified skincare and haircare formulator trained at Formula Botanica and other leading institutions in the United Kingdom. With a deep passion for natural science and a genuine love for the craft of formulation, the idea behind Bathelicious took shape: create products that are honest, effective, and rooted in nature.

The journey into formulation wasn't just about learning the science of skincare and haircare — it was about understanding how every ingredient, every process, and every choice of packaging leaves a footprint, on our bodies and on the planet we live in.

The Philosophy

A brand built on two convictions

Bathelicious was envisioned around two guiding principles that continue to shape every product the brand creates.

Self-conscious care

Formulations that are gentle, safe, and genuinely good for your skin and hair, made with ingredients you can trust and understand.

Environment-conscious craft

A commitment to sustainability at every stage — from plant-based formulations to eco-friendly, reusable packaging that reduces harm to the planet.

The Promise

Grown from a mission, made for everyone

From the very first formulation, Bathelicious has stood for clean, conscious self-care — personal care products that don't compromise on performance or principles. The brand brings together plant-powered ingredients, cruelty-free practices, and sustainable packaging to offer skincare, haircare, and body care that feels good to use and does good in the process.

What began as a formulator's mission has grown into a brand that invites everyone to be part of a cleaner, more conscious way of caring for themselves and the planet.

"Skin and hair care that is kind to you, and kind to the Earth."
